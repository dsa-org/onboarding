# Customer Support Request Intake

**Owner:** Product  
**Applies to:** Customer Success, Sales, Product, and Engineering  
**Slack Channel:** `#support-request`

## Purpose

This process ensures that Customer Success, Product, and Engineering receive the information needed to quickly investigate customer questions, reproduce issues, and provide accurate, timely responses. Following a standardized intake process reduces unnecessary back-and-forth communication, improves collaboration across teams, and helps us resolve customer issues more efficiently.
